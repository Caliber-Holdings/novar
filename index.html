FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is a VAR, and why noVAR?

A value added reseller buys technology and resells it with its margin folded into one price. noVAR was created to challenge that model by making cost, options and incentives visible, so clients stay in control of what they buy.

What can noVAR procure?

Hardware, software and services for large scale businesses, all handled through one transparent and streamlined process.

What does transparency look like in practice?

You see what the technology actually costs and how each option compares, so you can make an informed decision instead of accepting a single bundled number.

Who is on the noVAR team?

Experienced technical professionals, not salespeople. The team spent years in the technology services sector and started noVAR after growing frustrated with how the traditional VAR system treated clients.

How does the first conversation work?

We talk through your environment, needs, goals and budget. From there we prepare data driven options for you to review. Nothing is decided for you.

How is noVAR paid?

Our margin is never hidden. We disclose it during the quoting process and negotiate it with you, so you always know what noVAR earns on every purchase.
